About Us

About Dr. Lifeboat

Dr. Lifeboat is an internationally trained medical graduate with vast teaching experience in AMC, PLAB, and FMGE pathways. With strong understanding of exam psychology, pattern trends, and student struggles, Dr. Lifeboat has mentored hundreds of students to success — especially those who've faced multiple setbacks.

Our goal is to rescue committed students from cycles of repeated failure and guide them to their final destination — SUCCESS. We provide high-quality, exam-focused preparation material, interactive quizzes, performance analytics, and expert mentorship every step of the way.

🚀

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and result-driven platform for international medical graduates, empowering them to achieve success in the AMC/PLAB exams and confidently begin their medical careers in Australia/UK. We envision a future where every aspiring doctor, regardless of background or location, has access to expert guidance, high-yield resources, and a supportive community.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide comprehensive, student-centred AMC/PLAB preparation through: expert-led coaching, high-quality exam-focused content, adaptive technology, personalised academic support, and a distraction-free collaborative environment. We help students not just pass exams, but build lasting confidence, clinical competence, and clarity.
